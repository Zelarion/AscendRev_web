# AscendRev Website Working Guidance

## Copy voice and standards

For all new or revised AscendRev website copy, write with the direct, practical,
business-focused tone used in Sir Rio's approved wording. State the business need,
explain how AscendRev responds, and make the intended outcome easy to understand.

- Use clear, natural sentences, active verbs, and a confident but measured voice.
- Prefer concrete operating language over slogans, filler, jargon, and inflated claims.
- Keep paragraphs concise and connect each statement to the work AscendRev performs.
- Preserve the existing site voice and terminology; do not imitate another company's
  wording, branding, or distinctive creative work.
- Do not invent results, guarantees, certifications, facilities, or service commitments.
  Keep claims within the approved offer and documented evidence.
- Preserve existing copy unless the request explicitly asks for a change. When adding
  copy, avoid repeating nearby headings, service lists, and calls to action.

Treat these as standing instructions for future AscendRev website work.

## Split-section layout consistency

For split image-and-copy sections, place the image in the left column and its
related copy in the right column on desktop. On narrow screens, stack the image
before the copy. Keep the image and copy left-aligned; only depart from this
layout when a specific page brief explicitly calls for another composition.
