## Website section reference

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: desktop website section concept for a corporate outsourcing team
Primary request: Create a polished visual design reference for an AscendRev section titled “How we screen our team members.” Show a calm, credible hiring process as a vertical sequence. The section should feel editorial and structured, with the candidate interview photograph on the left and a right-hand numbered timeline that appears to progress as the visitor scrolls. Keep the content understandable as a normal page without animation.
Scene/backdrop: Clean off-white page under a deep navy site header. No browser chrome.
Subject: A professional interview setting on the left; a thin vertical timeline on the right, ending with a clearly emphasized “Job Offer” step.
Style/medium: High-fidelity web design mockup, restrained corporate art direction, sharp readable hierarchy, natural photographic treatment for the interview image.
Composition/framing: Wide landscape desktop layout, left aligned, generous but efficient spacing. Image occupies the left column; title and process copy occupy the right column. Include all seven steps in this order: “Resume Screening”, “Phone Interview”, “Face-to-Face Screening”, “Skills & Technical Assessment”, “Client / Partner Final Interview”, “Background Check & Clearance”, “Job Offer”.
Lighting/mood: Bright, calm, assured, and professional.
Color palette: AscendRev deep navy, white, warm muted gold, and restrained steel blue; small green detail only if needed.
Materials/textures: Fine borders, subtle paper-white surfaces, no glassmorphism, no heavy shadows.
Text (verbatim): “How we screen our team members”; “Resume Screening”; “Phone Interview”; “Face-to-Face Screening”; “Skills & Technical Assessment”; “Client / Partner Final Interview”; “Background Check & Clearance”; “Job Offer”.
Constraints: Keep the provided wording exact and legible; “Job Offer” is the last step. Show an optional scroll-progress cue, not a carousel or horizontal scroller. Keep the image on the left and copy on the right on desktop; stack image first on mobile. The design must still read clearly with motion disabled.
Avoid: Invented logos, invented certifications, guaranteed hiring outcomes, extra process stages, tiny unreadable copy, stock-photo watermarks, noisy gradients, oversized floating panels.
```
